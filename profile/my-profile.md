# My video profile

Claude reads this before every edit, and it wins over the rule book
(`rules/video-rules.md`). While `status` above says `template`, Claude stops
and asks you to fill it in before editing anything.

The easiest way: paste your content strategy, a description of your videos
and anything you already know about your style into the chat, and Claude
fills in the sections below for you. Write in your own words. Leave a section
as "none" if it doesn't apply, but answer every one.

Each section has a prompt (what to write) and an example (to show the kind
of answer, not to copy).

## 1. Who I am and what I'm building or selling

> Prompt: your name or handle, what you do, and the product, service or
> idea your videos are for.
>
> Example: "Sam, a nurse who runs a meal-prep business for shift workers.
> The videos are for my recipe app."

## 2. My audience

> Prompt: who you want watching, what they care about, and the words they
> would type into search when looking for something like you.
>
> Example: "Night-shift nurses and junior doctors, 22 to 35. They search
> 'night shift meals', 'meal prep for nurses', 'healthy shift snacks'."

<!-- fill in -->

## 3. My content strategy and goal

> Prompt: what the videos are for right now, what counts as a win, and any
> plan or schedule you follow. Paste your strategy as it is if you have one.
>
> Example: "Goal: 500 app signups by March. One filmed video a day: three
> founder-story videos and four recipe videos a week. Every video ends by
> pointing to the link in bio."

<!-- fill in -->

## 4. My content types

> Prompt: the kinds of video you make (for example desk talking head,
> talking while walking, day in the life, screen recording with voice, or
> your own), and for each one how long the headline should stay on screen.
>
> Example: "Desk talking head: headline the whole video. Walking: headline
> for the first 5 seconds. Kitchen day in the life: headline on the first
> scene only."

<!-- fill in -->

## 5. My style

> Prompt: organic or polished; how your headlines and hooks sound; how the
> captions should look; anything you never want on screen.
>
> Example: "Raw and honest, no fancy effects. Headlines in capitals, a
> confession or a number. White captions under my chin, never animated."

<!-- fill in -->

## 6. Words I use and words I never use

> Prompt: words, spellings and names to always use, and words never to use
> in on-screen text and captions.
>
> Example: "Always 'shift workers', never 'night owls'. My app is spelled
> 'PrepShift'. No emojis, no exclamation marks."

<!-- fill in -->

## 7. Word list for transcription

> Prompt: names Whisper should expect (your product, tools, places, people
> you mention), written as one or two short sentences, and any spellings the
> captions must fix.
>
> Example: "I built PrepShift for nurses. I use Notion and Canva." Fix:
> "prep shift" becomes "PrepShift".

<!-- fill in -->

## 8. What is true today, and claims I must never make

> Prompt: the facts about your product that videos can state, and the claims
> that are not true or not allowed (features not built yet, promises,
> prices, endorsements). Claude checks every claim you make on camera against
> this list (rule 40).
>
> Example: "True: free to try, works on iPhone and Android. Never claim: that
> it is a medical diet, a price, or that any hospital uses it."

<!-- fill in -->

## 9. Needs approval before posting

> Prompt: people, organisations or topics that must approve a video before
> it goes out, and names that must stay off screen until they do (rule 41).
>
> Example: "Anything that shows or names my hospital needs my manager's OK."

<!-- fill in -->

## 10. My call to action and link

> Prompt: what you ask viewers to do, in your own words, and where your link
> goes.
>
> Example: "'Link in bio, it's free to try.' The bio link goes to my sign-up
> page."

## 11. Screens and images I can show

> Prompt: where product screens come from (a demo or sample account, never
> real customer data), which screens are ready to show, and what must never
> appear (rule 37).
>
> Example: "Only the demo account. Desktop screens only until the new phone
> design ships. Never a real customer's name or order."

<!-- fill in -->

## 12. Platforms and posting schedule

> Prompt: where you post, how often, and the target length of a video.
>
> Example: "TikTok and Instagram Reels, one a day, 30 to 60 seconds."
